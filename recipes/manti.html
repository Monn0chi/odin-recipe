Manti

Description

Traditional Central Asian steamed dumplings filled with juicy spiced lamb and onion. Soft on the outside and full of flavor, they’re usually served with sour cream or yogurt.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups (360 g) all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup (240 ml) warm water
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 lb (450 g) ground lamb or beef
  • 2 large onions, finely chopped
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 2 tablespoons butter, melted
  • Sour cream or yogurt, for serving

Steps

  1. Mix the flour, water, and salt into a firm dough. Knead for 8 minutes, then cover and rest for 30 minutes.
  2. Combine the meat, onions, salt, pepper, and cumin in a bowl.
  3. Roll the dough thin and cut into 4-inch (10 cm) squares.
  4. Place a spoonful of filling in the center of each square, then pinch the corners together to seal.
  5. Grease a steamer rack and arrange the manti on it without touching.
  6. Steam over boiling water for 40 to 45 minutes until the dough is tender and the filling is cooked through.
  7. Brush with melted butter and serve hot with sour cream or yogurt.
