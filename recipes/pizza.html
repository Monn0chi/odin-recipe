Pizza Margherita

Description

A simple, classic pizza with a chewy, golden crust, tangy tomato sauce, and melted mozzarella, finished with fresh basil. Quick to make and always a crowd-pleaser.

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 cups (300 g) all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon instant yeast
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 3/4 cup (180 ml) warm water
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1/2 cup (120 g) tomato sauce
  • 1 1/2 cups (170 g) shredded mozzarella
  • Fresh basil leaves

Steps

  1. Mix the flour, yeast, salt, and sugar in a bowl. Add the warm water and olive oil and stir until a dough forms.
  2. Knead for 8 to 10 minutes until smooth, then cover and let rise for 1 hour.
  3. Preheat the oven to 475°F (245°C), with a baking sheet or pizza stone inside.
  4. Roll or stretch the dough into a 12-inch (30 cm) round on a floured surface.
  5. Spread the tomato sauce over the dough, leaving a small border, then top with the mozzarella.
  6. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbling.
  7. Top with fresh basil, slice, and serve hot.
