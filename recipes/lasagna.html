Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian baked pasta dish built from wide, flat noodles layered with rich meat ragù, creamy béchamel or ricotta, and plenty of melted cheese. Each layer bakes together into a tender, sliceable casserole with a golden, bubbling top and crisp, caramelized edges. It’s the kind of meal that fills the kitchen with the smell of tomatoes, garlic, and herbs long before it comes out of the oven.

This recipe takes a little time, but most of it is hands-off. A slow-simmered tomato and beef sauce develops deep flavor, while the cheese filling adds a smooth, mellow contrast. Assemble it ahead of time, let it rest for ten minutes after baking so the layers set, and serve with a crisp green salad and crusty bread. It's ideal for family dinners and gatherings, and the leftovers taste even better the next day.

Ingredients

Meat sauce

  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 lb (450 g) ground beef
  • 1/2 lb (225 g) Italian sausage, casings removed
  • 1 can (28 oz / 800 g) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 can (6 oz / 170 g) tomato paste
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon sugar

Cheese filling

  • 15 oz (425 g) ricotta cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 cup (50 g) grated Parmesan
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt

Assembly

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 4 cups (450 g) shredded mozzarella
  • 1/4 cup (25 g) extra grated Parmesan, for topping

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles in salted boiling water according to the package directions, then drain and lay them flat on a sheet of parchment or foil so they don’t stick together.
  3. Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for 4 to 5 minutes until soft, then add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
  4. Add the ground beef and sausage and cook until browned, breaking up the meat as it cooks. Drain off any excess fat.
  5. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, basil, oregano, salt, pepper, and sugar. Simmer on low heat for 20 to 30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  6. In a bowl, mix the ricotta, egg, Parmesan, parsley, and salt until well combined.
  7. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce over the bottom of a 9x13 inch (23x33 cm) baking dish.
  8. Layer 4 noodles over the sauce, then spread half of the ricotta mixture on top, followed by one third of the mozzarella and one third of the remaining meat sauce.
  9. Repeat with another layer of 4 noodles, the remaining ricotta mixture, one third of the mozzarella, and another third of the sauce.
  10. Finish with the last 4 noodles, the remaining sauce, the remaining mozzarella, and the extra Parmesan.
  11. Cover the dish with foil (lightly greased so the cheese doesn’t stick) and bake for 25 minutes.
  12. Remove the foil and bake for another 20 to 25 minutes, until the top is golden and bubbling.
  13. Let the lasagna rest for 10 to 15 minutes before slicing so the layers set. Serve warm.
