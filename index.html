Odin Recipe

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian baked pasta dish built from wide, flat noodles layered with rich meat ragù, creamy béchamel or ricotta, and plenty of melted cheese. Each layer bakes together into a tender, sliceable casserole with a golden, bubbling top and crisp, caramelized edges. It’s the kind of meal that fills the kitchen with the smell of tomatoes, garlic, and herbs long before it comes out of the oven.

This recipe takes a little time, but most of it is hands-off. A slow-simmered tomato and beef sauce develops deep flavor, while the cheese filling adds a smooth, mellow contrast. Assemble it ahead of time, let it rest for ten minutes after baking so the layers set, and serve with a crisp green salad and crusty bread. It's ideal for family dinners and gatherings, and the leftovers taste even better the next day.
